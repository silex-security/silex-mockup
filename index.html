Definitions

Draft definitions from GPT and product notes. Status: needs fact check before external review.

Residual risk

Prohibited outcomes still reachable through alternative paths after the observed attack was blocked. Open: measured after the policy is enforced, and over what window?

Residual reachability

Share of known prohibited-outcome paths still reachable under do(current control).

Sim-to-real calibration

Agreement between simulated and observed workflow behavior and outcomes; drifts as agents and workflows are added.

Coverage confidence

How much of the path, scenario, and environment space the current evidence covers.

Critical evidence

Production evidence tied to a critical prohibited outcome, such as a financial side effect, that needs immediate validation.

World model coverage

Completeness of the enterprise model by semantic dimension. Low coverage means more agents and data should be connected.

Defense confidence

Weighted confidence that current controls close the causal mechanism, given the quality of the evidence.
